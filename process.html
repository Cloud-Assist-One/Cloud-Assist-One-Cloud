Process

All Your Clouds, One View

A step-by-step look at the Cloud Cost Assistant portal — from signing in to raising a support ticket.

01

Sign in to your portal

Every client gets their own secure login. Your data is scoped to your company, so you only ever see your own clouds and your own spend.

02

See every cloud in one place

AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud and Snowflake each get their own tab, with the billing month front and centre. No more logging into four consoles to answer one question.

03

Pull billing straight from the provider

Pull a month's costs directly from your cloud account, or upload a billing export. Daily spend and cost by service are charted for you — month after month.

04

Act on live resources

See what is actually running right now, colour coded by how recently it appeared, with its Billing Code tag alongside. Verify a new resource is legitimate before it turns into next month's surprise.

05

Compare your clouds side by side

Put providers next to each other by category and see where the money actually goes, rather than comparing four differently shaped invoices by hand.

06

Drill down to the line item

Follow any number down to the individual resource and day it came from, and print a report by Billing Code when finance asks who the spend belongs to.

07

Bring your own billing files

Have a spreadsheet from a provider we don't pull from yet? Upload it and it is analysed alongside everything else, so the view stays complete.

Need a hand with tagging, billing optimization, or cloud technical support? Raise a ticket from inside the portal and a senior engineer picks it up.
